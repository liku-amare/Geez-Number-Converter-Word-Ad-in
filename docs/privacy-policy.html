Ge’ez Numerals Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Ge’ez Numerals is an offline utility that converts positive decimal numbers to traditional Ethiopic numerals.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or device data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network access. Numbers entered into the app remain on the user’s device.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not retain user-entered numbers. Clearing the input or closing the app removes them from the app interface.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Contact

For privacy questions, email samimanamare@gmail.com or open a support request in the app’s public issue tracker. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
