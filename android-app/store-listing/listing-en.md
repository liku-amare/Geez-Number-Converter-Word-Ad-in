## Short description

## Full description

Ge’ez Numerals is a focused, private, and offline converter for traditional Ethiopic numbers.

Enter a positive decimal number and instantly see its Ethiopic numeral representation. Copy the
result for use in messages, documents, notes, and learning materials.

Features:

- Converts positive decimal integers to Ethiopic numerals
- Supports positive integers up to 120 decimal digits
- Copies results with one tap
- Works completely offline
- Includes dark mode
- Contains no ads, analytics, accounts, or tracking

Traditional Ethiopic numerals do not use zero, so the converter accepts positive whole numbers.
